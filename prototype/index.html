YOUR FIRST RIDE

Let your movement guide the trail.

  1. On a phone, choose Connect phone motion, allow access, and calibrate while holding still.
  2. On a computer, choose Desktop controls, calibrate, and use the five large movement buttons.
  3. Begin the ride and follow each prompt. The horse, path, ground, and progress ring respond to your input.
  4. Continue after each successful challenge. You can pause or skip when needed.

Desktop input is simulated and does not validate phone recognition. The optional KNN lab is exploratory until it is evaluated with separate test examples.

Keep a secure grip and clear space. Small seated movements work. Stop if uncomfortable.
